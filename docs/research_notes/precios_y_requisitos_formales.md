# Precios de referencia y requisitos formales para un trabajador independiente de plomería, electricidad y reparaciones generales en CABA (noviembre 2026)

Reading notes for the report writer:

- All peso figures are **labor only (mano de obra), materials excluded**, unless stated otherwise. Each one carries its month/year.
- Source quality varies a lot. The only association tariff I could read first-hand is **AAIERIC (September 2026, PDF extracted directly)**. Most other per-task prices come from **Servidos** (a commercial marketplace that sells leads to tradespeople) and from **iProfesional** articles that relay **Home Solution** (a home-services company) and Clickie. These are market ranges, not official tariffs.
- Items marked **[snippet]** come from a search-result summary and were not opened and verified on the page itself.
- I found no price data dated October or November 2026 except one painting reference. Everything from mid-2026 needs an inflation adjustment (see the inflation section).

## 1. Reference price table (CABA/AMBA, 2026)

### Takeaway
There are two price levels in the market: the AAIERIC association tariff (visita $56.393, hora $56.393, urgencia nocturna/feriado mínimo $135.198, September 2026) and the marketplace/press ranges, which sit at roughly a third to a half of that (visita $17.000–$55.000, hora de "marido de alquiler" $22.500–$40.000, mid-2026). Plumbing has no association tariff that I could find; only market ranges exist.

**F. How the AAIERIC table is built**

- It is headed "Precios de mercado según encuesta a Instaladores Electricistas Profesionales. Tarifas aplicables a CABA y GBA", with values "referenciales para instalaciones de 220/380V residenciales y pequeños comercios hasta 10 kW". — [AAIERIC](https://aaieric.org.ar/costos-mano-de-obra)

### Inferences
- The AAIERIC hora ($56.393) is about 1,4 to 2,5 times the Servidos handyman hour ($22.500–$40.000). A multi-trade worker can reasonably quote electrical work off the AAIERIC table (and say so to the client) and generic handyman tasks off the lower market range.
- The AAIERIC ventilador de techo ($112.590) is well above the press range ($47.000–$76.000). The association figure equals its "service mínimo", so it behaves as a minimum call-out charge, not a pure task price.
- The UOCRA and AAIERIC jornales are wages for employed labor. They are a floor for what a helper costs, not what an independent charges a household.
- The Home Solution visita range for plumbers ($19.310–$83.902) is very wide; the top end probably covers camera or leak-detection diagnosis, but the article does not say.

### Gaps
- **No plumbing or sanitary association tariff found** (no Cámara de instaladores sanitaristas, SGBATOS or plumbers' union list surfaced). Plumbing prices are market ranges only.
- No reference price found for: instalación de bidet, instalación de termotanque/calefón a gas (gasista rates), colocación de cortinas, albañilería menor, a generic "marido de alquiler" day rate, or a percentage surcharge for night/weekend plumbing calls.
- ACYEDE, APSE and CADIME price lists were not found. The Electro Instalador magazine (September 2026 issue, [PDF](https://eba.electroinstalador.com/pdf/ei-240-septiembre-2026.pdf)) reportedly has a labor cost section; I did not open it.
- Revista Vivienda, Clarín ARQ and Mercado Libre price guides were not checked.
- No figures dated November 2026. The AAIERIC list for October/November probably exists on the same page but the file served was September.

## 2. Price movement 2025–2026, update practice, payment methods and factura

### Takeaway
The AAIERIC list is revised periodically with a stated variation (3,84% in the September 2026 edition), and press and marketplace guides advise reviewing prices every one or two months. I found no source describing dollar-linked pricing for small home repairs, and no data on payment-method shares.

### Cited Findings
- AAIERIC's September 2026 list states "Variación con anterior: 3.84%". — [AAIERIC](https://aaieric.org.ar/costos-mano-de-obra)
- The AAIERIC suggested value for tablero principal monofásico (1 ID + 1 TM + PAT) was $314.338 in June 2026 [snippet, second-hand citation] and $332.597 in September 2026. — [Servidos, tablero eléctrico](https://servidos.ar/blog/cuanto-cuesta-tablero-electrico-buenos-aires); [AAIERIC](https://aaieric.org.ar/costos-mano-de-obra)

### Inferences
- From June to September 2026 the AAIERIC tablero value rose about 5,8% (314.338 to 332.597), close to 2% a month. If that pace held, September figures need roughly +4% to reach November 2026, and June figures roughly +10%. This is my arithmetic, not a published index.
- Home Solution's flat range between June and September suggests some published "market" ranges are refreshed slowly; treat them as lagging.
- Monthly or bimonthly repricing in pesos, anchored on an association table where one exists, is the documented practice. Dollar-linking was not documented in any source I read.

### Gaps

- No data on the share of jobs paid in cash versus transfer or Mercado Pago, on cuotas, or on discounts for cash. No survey on how often households ask for factura.

## 3. What a solo multi-trade worker can realistically bill

### Takeaway
No source publishes income for independent tradespeople; the Servidos guide says explicitly that no official body does. Any monthly figure has to be built from hourly and per-job references.

### Cited Findings

Travel, unpaid quotes, tools, monotributo and insurance come out of that.
- Annualized, $2,0–$4,4 million a month is $24–$53 million, which lands in monotributo categories C to G on the August 2026 scale (see section 7). The tax category is therefore a real planning variable.
- For comparison, a UOCRA oficial especializado at 176 hours a month earns about $1,08 million basic plus non-remunerative sums (mid-2026).

## 4. Gas: who may do it, penalties, how to get the matrícula

### Takeaway
Gas work is the one area where a matrícula is unambiguously mandatory: it is issued by the distributor (Metrogas in CABA) under ENARGAS rules, and without it the distributor will not approve or enable the installation. A non-matriculado should not connect gas appliances, including a termotanque or calefón a gas.

### Cited Findings
- The gasista matrícula is mandatory, issued by the gas distributor (Metrogas, Naturgy) under ENARGAS rules, and valid in that distributor's zone. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Installing termotanques, calefones and any natural gas work requires a gasista matriculado; plumbers should not connect gas appliances. — [Servidos guía plomero](https://servidos.ar/guias-oficios/plomero); [Servidos, aire acondicionado](https://servidos.ar/blog/cuanto-cuesta-instalar-aire-acondicionado-2026)
- Course lengths: 3ra categoría (unifuncional) 404 hours; 2da categoría (domiciliario) 577 hours, the latter under Res. CFE 246/2015. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Metrogas offers courses toward the 2da or 3ra categoría matrícula and signed an agreement with the Ministerio de Capital Humano to train gasistas matriculados. — [EconoJournal](https://econojournal.com.ar/actualidad-mineria-energia/metrogas-y-el-ministerio-de-capital-humano-firman-un-convenio-para-formar-gasistas-matriculados/); [Metrogas, convenio (PDF)](https://www.metrogas.com.ar/assets/media/2026/03/Convenio-con-MCH-y-MetroGAS-ok-1-1.pdf)
- Metrogas registration paperwork: signed presolicitud form, copy of the matrícula, DNI, CUIT/CUIL and proof of annual payment; renewal through the AVM platform [snippet]. — [Metrogas, Gestión de matrículas (PDF)](https://www.metrogas.com.ar/assets/media/2022/08/Gestion-de-matriculas.pdf)
- Renewal is annual, from 2 January to 31 March, under NAG-200 and each distributor's requirements. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- A matriculado can be registered with only one distributor at a time, and registration as monotributista or responsable inscripto is required [snippet, unofficial guide]. — [Servidos, cómo ser gasista matriculado](https://servidos.ar/blog/como-ser-gasista-matriculado-en-argentina)
- Penalties: the regulator can impose fines scaled by severity (no amounts given); accidents may lead to criminal liability and insurers may reject claims. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- A blog mentions fines that "pueden superar $1.000.000" without citing a norm [snippet; unsupported]. — [Servidos, cómo ser gasista matriculado](https://servidos.ar/blog/como-ser-gasista-matriculado-en-argentina)
- Res. ENARGAS 219/2022, which required accident and liability insurance for gasistas, was repealed by Res. 21/2025; insurance now depends on each distributor's rules. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)

### Inferences
- For a solo multi-trade worker without matrícula, the commercially safe position is to exclude gas explicitly (and say so in the offer) or subcontract a matriculado. The 3ra categoría course (404 hours) is the shortest path to offering appliance connection legally.
- The practical penalty is less the fine than the consequences: no enabling by Metrogas, uninsured liability, and criminal exposure if there is an accident.

### Gaps
- I did not verify on ENARGAS or Metrogas primary pages the exact scope of each category (1ra, 2da, 3ra), the current cost of the matrícula and annual fee, or the sanction regime with its norm number. All gas findings above except the Metrogas documents and EconoJournal come from a commercial guide.
- The Servidos page refers to the regulator as "ENReGE (ex-ENARGAS)"; I did not confirm the regulator's current name or status.

## 5. Electricity: is a matrícula required in CABA?

### Takeaway
In CABA the register for non-degree electricians is COPIME's Registro de Idóneos; what is clearly reserved is **signing documents** (DCI and similar), which under Res. ENRE 269/12 requires a professional, technician or installer with officially homologated training. I found no norm that plainly prohibits an unregistered person from doing minor residential repairs, and sources disagree on which body governs.

### Cited Findings
- Res. COPIME 95/2026 (dated 28/04/2026, published in the Boletín Oficial on 11/05/2026, aviso N° 30861/26) keeps registering idóneos instaladores electricistas and fixes their scope. — [Derecho en Zapatillas](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/matricula-de-electricista/); [Boletín Oficial 11/05/2026](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/pdf/aviso/primera/341747/20260511)
- Under that resolution: holders of the CIMEBA course certificate (Instalador Electricista Nivel 3, 250 hours) are registered as provisional and become definitive on passing COPIME's validation exam; holders of courses of at least 250 hours from ACYEDE, MONACO, NEWTON, MILAN and AULA 365 (taken since 2006, on AEA 90364 part 7 section 771) are registered as definitive after the validation exam. — [Derecho en Zapatillas](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/matricula-de-electricista/)
- People registered on those certificates **cannot issue DCI** or other documents contrary to Res. ENRE 269/12, until the training is officially homologated (arts. 4 to 7). — [Derecho en Zapatillas](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/matricula-de-electricista/)
- Res. ENRE 269/12, as cited by COPIME: DCIs must be signed by professionals, technicians or installers with specific competence homologated by the education authority and registered with the professional or technical college of the jurisdiction. — [Derecho en Zapatillas](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/matricula-de-electricista/)
- Legal basis of COPIME: Decreto 6070/58 ratified by Ley 14.467; the Registro de Idóneos was opened by Res. COPIME 07/68. — [Derecho en Zapatillas](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/matricula-de-electricista/)
- The Nivel 3 idóneo register covers installations up to 10 kW in CABA; the provisional registration lasts one year. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- COPIME annual fee for technicians and idóneos: $135.000 a year in two installments of $67.500 (Res. COPIME 123/2026, until 31/10/2026); from 1/11/2026, $146.250 a year with a second installment of $73.125 (Res. COPIME 189/2026). Two consecutive unpaid years lead to suspension. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Another source estimates about $202.500 a year in CABA [snippet]; the two figures conflict. — [Servidos, cómo ser electricista](https://servidos.ar/blog/como-ser-electricista-en-buenos-aires)
- What is commonly done without registration, per the commercial guide: appliance-level work (tomas, interruptores, luminarias). It says tableros, breakers, fault diagnosis in fixed installations and anything requiring a signature need a matrícula, and that the AGC can impose fines (no amounts). — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Another page from the same site puts it more loosely: "sin registro podés hacer reparaciones; los trabajos de firma (planos, certificaciones, habilitaciones) exigen registro vigente" [snippet]. — [Servidos guía electricista](https://servidos.ar/guias-oficios/electricista)
- Course lengths cited: UTN Buenos Aires electrician 252 hours; Electricista en Inmuebles 540 hours; Montador Tablerista 660 hours. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- AAIERIC prices DCI certificates with a note "Referencia CTPBA" (the Province's technicians' college), i.e. its DCI reference is for Provincia, not CABA. — [AAIERIC](https://aaieric.org.ar/costos-mano-de-obra)
- AAIERIC states that all materials used must be certified to the corresponding IRAM/IEC standard. — [AAIERIC](https://aaieric.org.ar/costos-mano-de-obra)

### Inferences
- The two Servidos pages contradict each other on whether changing a térmica or disyuntor requires registration. The firm part is narrower: a non-registered or idóneo electrician cannot sign a DCI, so any job that needs one (new supply, medidor, reconnection after a cut by Edenor/Edesur, habilitaciones) must go through someone with the right credentials.
- For display purposes, a COPIME idóneo registration number is a real, checkable credential obtainable with a 250-hour course plus exam and about $146.250 a year (from November 2026). It does not allow signing DCIs.

### Gaps
- **Sources conflict on the governing body in CABA**: COPIME, the AGC, or "GCBA" are each named by different commercial guides. Only COPIME's role is backed by a published resolution. I found no official AGC page on an electricians' register.
- I did not read Res. ENRE 269/12, Ley 19.587 or the AEA 90364 text directly, nor Edenor/Edesur pages on when a DCI is demanded. APSE requirements were not found.
- No primary source found stating what an unregistered person may or may not legally do inside a dwelling.

## 6. Plumbing and sanitary work

### Takeaway
Ordinary plumbing repairs in CABA need no matrícula. A registered instalador sanitarista is needed only for connection to the water or sewer network and for approving sanitary plans, and sources disagree on which body runs that register.

### Cited Findings
- Plumbing repair (destapaciones, pérdidas, grifería, inodoros, cañerías internas) generally requires no professional license in CABA or most of Buenos Aires Province. — [Servidos guía plomero](https://servidos.ar/guias-oficios/plomero); [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Connecting to the water or sewer network and approving sanitary plans require a registered instalador sanitarista; the page says no public registry was operating as of September 2026. — [Servidos guía plomero](https://servidos.ar/guias-oficios/plomero)
- The same site elsewhere names the issuing body as the ERAS registry ("continúa la ex matrícula de OSN") for the AySA area. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Other pages claim two licenses coexist in CABA (an AySA one for network connection and an AGC "Instalador Sanitario" one for signing internal installations), while another says AySA does not issue a matrícula at all and places the register at COPIME [snippets; contradictory]. — [Servidos, exámenes plomero](https://servidos.ar/examenes-gratis/plomero); [Servidos, verificador matrícula plomero](https://servidos.ar/verificador-matricula-plomero)
- A 2023 national guide says internal installations must be carried out by "personal idóneo". — [Dirección Nacional de Agua Potable y Saneamiento (PDF)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/if-2023-141050544-apn-dnapysmop-guia.pdf) [snippet]
- Trades with no mandatory matrícula per the guide: plomero (repairs), albañil, pintor, carpintero, cerrajero, jardinero, colocador de cerámicos. Work above 2 m requires fall protection under Ley 19.587 and Decreto 911/96. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Split air conditioning: no gasista matrícula is needed (it is refrigerant work, not natural gas), and there is no specific national matrícula for AC technicians; a new electrical line is a separate job. — [Servidos, aire acondicionado](https://servidos.ar/blog/cuanto-cuesta-instalar-aire-acondicionado-2026); [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)

### Inferences
- For a repair-and-maintenance business, the sanitarista matrícula is irrelevant day to day. It only matters if the worker wants new network connections or plan sign-off, which is construction work.

### Gaps
- No official AySA or ERAS page describing the current register, requirements or cost was found. The body in charge is unresolved.

## 8. Insurance: accidentes personales, responsabilidad civil, ART

### Takeaway
A monotributista cannot take out an ART for themselves; the product consorcios and companies ask for is a seguro de accidentes personales with a cláusula de no repetición endorsed to the client's CUIT. I found no published premium for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- There is no legal "ART para monotributistas": ART belongs to employment relationships (Ley 24.557). The substitute is accident insurance, which usually pays a fixed capital on death or disability. — [Asegurar Directo](https://www.asegurardirecto.com.ar/segurosonline/seguro-de-accidentes-personales-para-monotributistas-seguro-de-trabajo/)
- Typical requirements from contracting companies and consorcios: cláusula de no repetición (the insurer waives its right to claim back from the contracting party), the contracting party's CUIT included in the policy, and the insured's name, DNI, CUIT and declared activity, which must match the actual task. — [Asegurar Directo](https://www.asegurardirecto.com.ar/segurosonline/seguro-de-accidentes-personales-para-monotributistas-seguro-de-trabajo/)
- Coverage: accidental death and total or partial permanent disability during work, commuting (in itinere), 24-hour or working-hours scope, nationwide; policies can be taken by the day for short jobs. — [Asegurar Directo](https://www.asegurardirecto.com.ar/segurosonline/seguro-de-accidentes-personales-para-monotributistas-seguro-de-trabajo/)
- Insured sums commonly requested by clients range from $20.000.000 to $80.000.000 depending on the specification, covering accidental death, permanent disability, medical-pharmaceutical assistance and funeral costs [snippet]. — [Asegurar Directo, construcción](https://www.asegurardirecto.com.ar/segurosonline/seguros-para-la-construccion-precio-seguro-para-albaniles-y-clausula-de-no-repeticion/)
- Plans are sold by the day, week or month, and the cost varies slightly with the number of beneficiaries added in the endorsement [snippet]. — [Asegurar Directo, tag no repetición](https://www.asegurardirecto.com.ar/segurosonline/tag/seguro-de-accidentes-personales-con-clausula-de-no-repeticion/)
- The broker advises consorcios to make the insurance a contractual requirement for gardeners and maintenance staff and to ask for the coverage certificate before work starts [snippet]. — [Asegurar Directo, jardineros y mantenimiento](https://www.asegurardirecto.com.ar/segurosonline/seguro-de-accidentes-personales-para-jardineros-y-personal-de-mantenimiento/)
- The trades guide recommends accident and liability insurance for all trades "ya que muchos consorcios y clientes lo exigen". — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Example of the liability at stake: an August 2026 ruling upheld a damages claim against a consorcio and the owner of a lift maintenance firm over a fatal lift accident [snippet]. — [Microjuris](https://aldiaargentina.microjuris.com/2026/08/24/fallos-accidente-fatal-procedencia-de-una-demanda-de-danos-contra-un-consorcio-y-contra-el-titular-de-una-empresa-conservadora-por-el-fallecimiento-de-una-nina-en-un-accidente-con-un-ascensor-del-ed/)

### Inferences
- Nearly all insurance findings come from one broker with a commercial interest. The clause description is standard, but the "everyone requires it" framing should be read as sales copy; one of its own pages concedes it is not a universal legal requirement.
- Because policies can be bought by the day or month and endorsed per client CUIT, a solo worker can hold a base monthly policy and add endorsements as consorcios ask.

### Gaps
- **No 2026 premium found** for accident insurance or for responsabilidad civil. Broker pages only offer online quotes.
- No primary source (administrators' chamber, CABA Registro Público de Administradores, or a norm) stating what an administrador must demand from providers. Whether this is legal obligation or custom remains unverified.
- Nothing found on responsabilidad civil policy terms or typical limits for tradespeople.

## 9. How much credentials weigh commercially

### Takeaway
I found no quantitative evidence (survey, experiment or marketplace data) that showing a matrícula, factura, written warranty or insurance raises conversion or price. The only support is qualitative: consorcios, administraciones, comercios and inmobiliarias generally require factura and often insurance, while for households it is advice from guides.

### Cited Findings
- "Consorcios, administraciones, comercios e inmobiliarias" generally require a factura. — [Servidos guía plomero](https://servidos.ar/guias-oficios/plomero)
- Many consorcios and clients require accident and liability insurance. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Without invoice or signed quote, collecting payment is harder; trades without mandatory matrícula still carry civil liability for damage. — [Servidos, habilitaciones y matrículas](https://servidos.ar/blog/habilitaciones-matriculas-oficios-buenos-aires)
- Consumer-facing legal guidance tells clients to ask for proof of current registration or matrícula and check its scope before hiring an electrician. — [Derecho en Zapatillas](https://www.derechoenzapatillas.com/2026/matricula-de-electricista/)
- A 2026 price guide quotes its visit fee specifically for an "electricista matriculado" (ARS 15.000–35.000) [snippet], and marketplace listings use "matriculado" in ad titles. — [Roomix](https://roomix.ai/blog/cuanto-cuesta-electricista-2026)
- Marketplaces offer public matrícula checkers for gasistas and COPIME registrants. — [Servidos verificador gasista](https://servidos.ar/verificador-matricula-gasista); [Servidos verificador COPIME](https://servidos.ar/verificador-matricula-copime)
- A Metrogas–Capital Humano note reports that 89% (of a group not defined in the snippet) did not know they could obtain the professional matrícula from their secondary-school qualification [snippet]. — [EconoJournal](https://econojournal.com.ar/actualidad-mineria-energia/metrogas-y-el-ministerio-de-capital-humano-firman-un-convenio-para-formar-gasistas-matriculados/)

### Inferences
- Credentials act as an entry ticket for the consorcio and business segment (factura plus accident insurance with no-repetition clause), not as a proven price lever with households.
- The gap between the AAIERIC tariff and the generic market range (section 1) is consistent with registered, association-aligned electricians charging more, but that is correlation from two different sources, not evidence of a premium caused by the credential.
- The existence of public checkers means a displayed matrícula number can be verified; displaying a false or lapsed one is a risk.

### Gaps
- No data on conversion uplift, price premium, or household preferences regarding matrícula, factura, garantía escrita or insurance. This question is unanswered by available sources.

## 10. Free or low-cost training and displayable credentials in CABA

### Takeaway
GCBA's Centros de Formación Profesional offer free courses with official certificates, including electricity and gas, and the route to a displayable electrical credential is a 250-hour-plus course followed by COPIME's validation exam. Details below are mostly from secondary sources and need checking on buenosaires.gob.ar.

### Cited Findings
- GCBA has 58 Centros de Formación Profesional with more than 250 free courses for people over 18 with DNI, with official certification [snippet]. — [Servidos, escuelas de oficios](https://servidos.ar/escuelas-de-oficios); [BAE Negocios (2025)](https://www.baenegocios.com/amp/La-Ciudad-abrio-la-inscripcion-mas-de-250-cursos-con-rapida-salida-laboral-20250714-0052/)

### Inferences
- The cheapest credential stack for this profile: a free CFP certificate in electricity (displayable on its own), then COPIME idóneo registration if the course is one COPIME accepts, and optionally the 404-hour gas course for 3ra categoría. Whether a given CFP course qualifies for COPIME registration must be asked of COPIME, since Res. 95/2026 lists specific institutes.

### Gaps
- No confirmed GCBA plumbing/sanitary course, and no information found on IRAM certification of competencies for these trades.
